# Rejected: never pitch these again

| Date | What | Why / what instead |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-11 | Full-3D stick-figure character rig | The user didn't like the look. Use the prebuilt 2D toon cards, keeping the better physics and logic. |
| 2026-11 | Moving the reels to Blender | Parked. Improve the three.js + Remotion workflow instead. |
| 2026-11 | Drawn stand-in TCG cards | Every reel uses real card scans fetched for that story. |
| 2026-11 | Limbs that stretch to reach a prop (GPS hook and yank arms) | Limbs have one fixed length; the body moves instead. |
| 2026-11 | Hands, fingers, feet or shoes on the stick figures | Limbs are plain lines that just end. |
| 2026-11 | Flat stickers standing in for props (the PSA padlock) | Props are built objects with parts, thickness, weight and physics. |
| 2026-11 | A held screen turned to face the viewer | Only seen from the owner's side. |
| 2026-11-18 | Jaw drop as a long mouth from the chin to the floor (Mudkip S15, S19) | "why they touch the ground lol. be logical". The mouth gapes on the face and ends above the chin (`FrontOpts.gape`). |
| 2026-11 | Sparkles over a card face | Shrink and offset them, or drop them. |
| 2026-11-25 | Dull palettes: greys, near-black navy, muddy/desaturated purples, grey-blue night scenes, a blue-tinted grade | The client called the colours dull, and the user said the colour choices themselves were "dark colours with no soul". Use the bright reference palettes (rules.md section 0). |
| 2026-11-25 | Depth of field / lens blur / any blur | "I need no blurs." Every frame sharp edge to edge. |
| 2026-11-25 | Cast shadows | They came out inaccurate and ruined the video. No shadows at all. |
| 2026-11-25 | Tight, zoomed-in cameras cutting off characters and main subjects (about 80% of shots) | Medium-wide by default, a wide shot at each new location, close-ups about 1 in 4 at most. |
| 2026-11-25 | Sets laid out side by side for a landscape frame | Always vertical: stack subjects top to bottom or front to back. |
| 2026-11-25 | Bare box sets, empty walls, flat-colour skies | Dressed sets, plus blue skies with clouds. |
| 2026-11-25 | Custom-drawn graded slabs; low-res card textures | Real slab photos (label edits through Grok image_edit); scans of 1000 px or more drawn at twice their on-screen size. |
| 2026-11-25 | Higgsfield for anything | Too expensive; only when the user asks for it by name. |
| 2026-11-27 | Characters sitting (café chairs, match-table chairs, sitting on a suitcase) | "dont create chairs or scenes where our character have to sit … how bad it looks getting standing there like that" (iCaterpie f231, f459). Stage everyone standing. |
| 2026-11-27 | Complex metaphor machines the cast operates (the iCaterpie public scale: pans on chains, a swinging beam, weights carried by two lifters) | "this weighing machine was also badly animated, dont create complex scene if we can't actually make them well" (iCaterpie f1869). Simple one-motion gags only. |
