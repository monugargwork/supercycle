# Harrods / Westfield queue fights: facts
Sources:
- https://www.london-now.co.uk/news/26727729.pok-mon-fans-fight-harrods-westfield-upc-cards/ (syndicated USA Today piece by Joseph Reaidi, 13 Nov 2026)
- Product details: billsarchive.com 30th anniversary MSRP page; GameStop and Smyths listings.

**Single source.** No second outlet covers the London incident.

## Confirmed (London Now)
- 12 Nov 2026: fights broke out among people queuing for the Pokémon 30th Celebration Ultra-Premium Collection, inside Westfield Shepherd's Bush and outside Harrods, Knightsbridge.
- Met Police: there were no calls about Westfield. Multiple officers attended Harrods "after a few calls were made to manage the crowd". The spokesperson said: "Due to the number of people that turned up, the event was subsequently cancelled by Harrods." Also: "No arrests or dispersal orders were needed."
- "It is understood" (the article's wording, not attributed to police) that the disorder was caused by a few people unhappy about their place in the queue.
- Videos:
  - Westfield security guards try to drag apart young men who are pushing and hitting each other; people run and security chases them.
  - Pushing and grabbing in a queue.
  - A photo of people fighting outside Harrods.
- Background: in June, men fought with bats at Southernwood Retail Park, Old Kent Road; a 43-year-old man was arrested.

## Product (retail pages, not the article)
- UPC Day and Night editions, each with 29 booster packs plus a Classic Collection pack.
- Day box: foil Pikachu ex and Espeon ex promos. Night box: Pikachu ex and Umbreon ex promos, a playmat and sleeves.
- US price $179.99. UK price unconfirmed (£169.99 or £179). The article does not say which edition Harrods sold.
