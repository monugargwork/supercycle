## Logline
A card marketplace passes a billion dollars and throws itself a party, while its small sellers, squeezed by fees, banned tools and the platform's own copycat shop, write letters up to the CEO and the federal government; the boss changes, the new one praises "the community", a small shop owner asks for a seat at the table, and there still isn't one.

## Spine
1. TCGplayer (eBay's marketplace for Magic and Pokémon cards) passes $1B GMV; sellers answer by asking the government to investigate.
2. The party is upstairs; the sellers don't eat the cake: high fees, banned third-party tools, and TCGplayer's own shop selling right on top of them.
3. The Syracuse authentication center closed (May 2025), 200+ union jobs gone; sellers wrote to the CEO, then federal regulators; TCGplayer denied a lot and fixed a few programs.
4. "You see": a market where management charges your stall fee, bans your own scale, then builds an identical stall next to yours.
5. November: Rob Bigler out, Ron Jaiven in, praising the community's passion; a South Dakota shop owner: meet 50 small sellers, not just the biggest accounts.
6. The billion arrived; the sellers' chair is still missing.
