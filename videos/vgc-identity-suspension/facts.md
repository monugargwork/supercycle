# Chen-Wei Liu VGC suspension: facts
Sources:
- https://www.dexerto.com/pokemon/pokemon-vgc-competitor-claims-he-was-wrongfully-suspended-after-another-player-used-his-identity-at-worlds-3427140/ (10 Nov 2026)
- https://criticalhits.com.br/games/suspensao-de-chen-wei-liu-gera-contestacao-apos-disputa-de-identidade-no-mundial/ (repeats the same X timeline, so not independent)

**All of this comes from Liu's side, via his friend Johnny Chiou's X post. Neither TPC nor RK9 has commented publicly.**

## Timeline
- **2024 Worlds:** Liu used Player ID 5283777.
- **28 Aug 2026 (day one of Worlds VGC):** a match slip lists that ID under another player's name.
- **4 Sep:** Liu finds his RK9 profile and history gone.
- **5 Sep:** he contacts RK9.
- **17 Sep:** RK9 reportedly confirms the account was moved to a new email before Worlds, after a request with the correct credentials. Liu says he never asked for this.
- **20 Sep:** he asks TPCi to investigate.
- **16 Oct:** disciplinary notice DISINC-007883 suspends him indefinitely under Standards of Conduct 4.3, "Deception, defrauding, or engagement of a fellow program participant under false pretenses". The notice doesn't say what he did.
- **17 Oct:** he sends a rebuttal, then follows up around 22 Oct.
- **10 Nov:** there has still been no reply.
- **Appeal:** only possible after 90 days.
- RK9 is the registration and pairing platform for Pokémon events (general knowledge; not stated in the articles).

## Not named
The other player whose name appears on the slip. Neither article says he knowingly did anything wrong.
