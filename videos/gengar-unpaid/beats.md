## Logline
A Pristine 10 Gengar hammers at $576,000 on Fanatics Collect and is marked unpaid the next day. The market had already popped the champagne, so collectors learn to look for one word before celebrating: PAID.

## Spine
1. Hook: the gavel lands $576,000 on a Gengar, and next day the sale is marked unpaid and vanishes like a ghost.
2. What it is: 2002 Legendary Collection reverse holo, CGC Pristine 10. 32 bids on 11 Oct; if paid, a Gengar record far above $408k.
3. Two weeks later a Pristine 10 Latios Gold Star sells for $504k, the best of 170+. The market pops champagne.
4. Turn: if the winner hasn't paid, who pays for the champagne?
5. You see: an auction price is a "mai tao trả" promise. It turns real only when the money moves.
6. Proof: a news site finds millions of unpaid Pokémon results quietly erased from the sales history. Fanatics says it keeps the data accurate.
7. Payoff: collectors now check for ĐÃ TRẢ before popping, and Gengar is still grinning.
