## Logline
A seller lists a Jirachi at $20,000 under the name of Avery, a 12-year-old who opened packs on camera through his cancer treatment and pulled that Jirachi in his last video; the family asks for peace, the charity listings come down, and the card's artist only wanted her art to make people happy, while Avery only wanted to open it.

## Spine
1. A seller lists a Jirachi at $20,000, tied to a boy who just died.
2. Avery, 12, "Avery the Poke Kid", opened packs on camera through his treatment; in the last video he pulled that Jirachi.
3. 18 Sept he died; within a week listings appeared.
4. The "90% to charity" listing really gave 10%; the same card sells for $65-$240. A card, or a family's grief?
5. The parents: not involved, nothing received; take them down; it doesn't honour his heart; we mourn, let us be.
6. The charity listings come down; the artist wanted her art to bring happiness. $20,000 vs. a boy who just wanted to open it.
