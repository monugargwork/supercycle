# Retford wall-drilling theft: facts
Sources:
- https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cme3d2xq5ez3o (17 Nov 2026, read in full)
- https://hellorayo.co.uk/hits-radio/east-midlands/news/two-arrested-stolen-pokemon-playing-cards-retford (Dec 2025, read in full)

## Confirmed (BBC)
- Police say a group allegedly drilled through the wall of a business premises on Aurillac Way, Retford, and took 26 boxes of cards in December.
- Six men were charged with conspiracy to commit burglary between 25 and 28 December. They appeared at Mansfield Magistrates' Court on a Tuesday.
- Two appeared by video link from prison and were remanded in custody. Three attended in person and were bailed. A sixth, appearing virtually, was bailed too.
- Bail conditions include not contacting co-defendants and not going to "Obsidia Ltd, a trading card business in Retford".
- Plea hearing: Nottingham Crown Court, 15 December.

## Confirmed (Hello Rayo, Dec 2025)
- Just after 1 a.m. on Saturday 27 Dec 2025. The alarm alerted police.
- CCTV followed a van with cloned number plates toward South Yorkshire, then Manchester. Officers intercepted it at a storage yard; two men were arrested while removing boxes. Tools, including a sledgehammer, were in the van. The stolen goods were recovered.
- Det. Sgt Andrea Brown: "In a few hours of the burglary occurring, we have not only arrested two suspects but secured the stolen goods."
