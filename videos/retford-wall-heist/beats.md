# Retford wall heist: beats

VO 79.74 s (f0-2392), reel 2425 f. Frames are VO word onsets from `words.json`.

**Logline:** A gang drills through a card warehouse wall at 1 a.m. and gets away with 26 boxes, but a CCTV camera follows their fake-plated van all the way to Manchester, where two are caught unloading; eleven months later six men stand charged and are barred from the shop.

## Spine
1. They break in through the wall and take the boxes (S01-S03).
2. It was planned for days, yet the alarm catches them (S04-S06).
3. The real problem is the camera: a fake plate changes the name, not the van (S07-S10).
4. Police stop the van in Manchester, two arrested, goods back within hours (S11-S12).
5. Eleven months on: six charged, two held, four bailed and kept away from the shop (S13-S16).
6. They got through the wall, but can't come near the door (S17).

## Hero objects
- **The wall hole:** a ring of drilled holes, the brick plug falling in (S01). Stays open through S06; bricked up with newer, paler bricks in S17.
- **The van:** white van with an orange roof ladder and a dent in the sliding door. The dent and ladder are what the cameras recognise, whatever the plate says. Its fake plate is a printed plate taped over the real one, one tape corner lifting.
- **The 26 cartons:** plain brown cartons stencilled TRADING CARDS / FRAGILE (no brand logos). Always closed.

## Hero moments ★
- **S01, the hook:** the last drill hole, then the brick plug tips in and crashes on the punch word "tường" (rigid-body bricks, debris stays).
- **S07-S08, the camera:** the bell is small; tilt up to the CCTV camera, its REC lamp blinks on, the head swivels to track the van. Insert on the taped plate.
- **S11, the arrest:** dawn at the storage yard, a police car swings across the gate, the two men freeze, one carton drops and bounces (stays closed). Insert of the sledgehammer in the van.

## Sets and journeys
1. **Retford unit** (industrial estate): brick back wall with the hole; inside, shelves of cartons; front with a TRADING CARDS sign, a glass door whose window shows real cards, a wreath, the corner lamp post with the CCTV camera, the road opposite. Night (rich blue, moon, clouds, warm sodium lamps), then winter daylight in S17.
2. **Road out**: dual carriageway with blue motorway signs (SOUTH YORKSHIRE, then MANCHESTER) and CCTV poles. Night.
3. **Police control room**: wall of monitors and a big map of northern England. Warm lamps.
4. **Kid's room** ("you see" set): warm, a tall desk, the notebook.
5. **Manchester storage yard**: rows of orange and blue shipping containers, city towers behind, pink-orange dawn with clouds.
6. **Mansfield court**: brick-and-glass front with steps, a signpost, a notice board; inside, a wood-panelled courtroom with a glass dock and a wall TV. Bright autumn day.

Route: S01 hole → through the hole into the warehouse (S02) → follow the alarm cable up and over the roofs to the police station (S03) → crane down to the wide of the estate (S04) → through the van's windscreen (S05) → hard cut to the hole (S06) → tilt up to the CCTV (S07) → follow the van onto the road (S08) → plate matches to the notebook label (S09) → notebook closes, match to the van on a control-room monitor (S10) → monitor route leads to the yard (S11-S12) → dissolve, 11 months (S13) → through the court doors (S14) → out onto the steps (S15-S16) → dissolve to the Retford unit in winter sun (S17).

## Cast (standing everyone; nobody sits)
- **Burglars A and B:** dark-green hoodies up, neck gaiters pulled up so only generic eyes show. A holds the drill; both carry one carton each.
- **Desk officer** (S03, S10): police uniform, standing.
- **Two arresting officers** (S11): hi-vis jackets.
- **Detective** (S12): plain coat, lanyard reading DETECTIVE. Not drawn as the real officer, not named on screen.
- **Six men** (S13-S16): plain suits in different colours, generic faces; no likenesses or names. Two only on the courtroom TV in grey prison sweatshirts.
- **Kid** (S09, and the customer in S17): school jumper.

## Visual laws
- **Being seen:** every CCTV camera's REC lamp blinks red the moment the van is in its view. One law for the whole reel, from S07 to S10.
- **The alarm's reach:** the bell rattles, and the camera follows its cable across the roofs to the police station, whose alarm lamp is flashing.
- **Time:** night (S01-S10) → dawn at the arrest (S11) → morning sun (S12) → autumn day (S13-S16) → winter sun (S17). Days of watching use a tear-off calendar on the van's dashboard (S05).

## Gags (each one simple motion)
- "Khoan…bị bắt quả tang?": the burglars freeze mid-carry in the hole, cartons in their arms, red alarm light washing over them, looking at the lens (Huh).
- "bìa vở": the kid peels the name label off the notebook and sticks on a new one; opens the cover: the old name is written at the top of every page.
- "không bén mảng tới… Retford": at the foot of the court steps a signpost points RETFORD; one man starts that way, stops, turns round and walks off the other way.
- Ending: the shop door opens for a kid, and the little door bell rings, rhyming with the alarm bell.

## Energy and light
- Peak 1 at S01 (the breach), peak 2 at S11 (the arrest). Stillness at S16 (the notice board, no motion but a slow push) before the last line.
