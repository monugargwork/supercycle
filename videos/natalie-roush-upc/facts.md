# Natalie Roush and the early 30th Celebration UPC: facts
Sources:
- https://www.dexerto.com/pokemon/youtuber-opens-unreleased-180-pokemon-box-then-promotes-2262-affiliate-listing-3407060/ (8 Sep 2026)
- https://kotaku.com/roush-pokemon-tcg-30th-celebration-set-stolen-cards-ultimate-premium-2000735067 (16 Sep 2026)
- https://www.dexerto.com/pokemon/pokemon-cracks-down-on-early-30th-anniversary-card-sales-over-stolen-property-concerns-3402079/ (25 Aug 2026)

## Confirmed
- **The video:** Roush opened the Night Ultra-Premium Collection (Umbreon) in a YouTube Short about two months before its 6 Nov 2026 launch. She also has the Day edition. Retail price is $179.99.
- **In the video:** two Magikarp cards, one valued at $320 raw.
- **The affiliate link:** YouTube tagged the Short "Earns commission", and a Shop button led to a GOAT listing at $2,262, more than 12x retail. She is an affiliate, not the seller.
- **Backlash:** a collector's tweet and a post by Peak Hobby; other creators criticised her; her Instagram filled with sarcasm. The video was later deleted.
- **Quotes (Kotaku):** "I chose to spend my own money on this." Peak Hobby (Dexerto): "Natalie Roush on her YouTube is selling you 30th Anniversary Celebration Night UPC for $2,262."
- **The wider picture:** The Pokémon Company said earlier leaked 30th products were "not distributed or made available for public sale through authorized channels". eBay removed 30th Celebration listings, saying "The sale of stolen property is not allowed on eBay."
- **Proof:** there is no evidence she stole the boxes or knew of any problem with their origin; how she got them is unclear.

## Not claimed
- That the boxes were stolen. Only that the community suspected it.
- Any Pokémon Company statement about her.

## Show
- A phone with a YouTube Short, an "Earns commission" tag, a Shop button with $2,262, Day and Night boxes, a deleted-video screen.
