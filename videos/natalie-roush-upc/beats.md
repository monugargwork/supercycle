# Natalie Roush (the early Night UPC): beats

VO: `voice_src.wav` → `voice.wav` (76.9 s, speech ends f2293). Reel 2330 f. Script as approved in `script.md`; facts in `facts.md`.

**Logline:** a Pokémon creator opens the 30th-anniversary Night Ultra-Premium Collection two months before release, with a $2,262 "Shop" button sitting right under the video; the community asks where an unreleased box came from, she says she paid with her own money, nothing is proven, but everyone saw the button.

## Spine
1. Hook: an unreleased box opened on camera, and a buy button for $2,200 right under it.
2. Who and what: a Pokémon creator, the 30th Ultra-Premium, Night edition, Umbreon. Retail $179.99, out 6 Nov.
3. In the video: two Magikarp, one priced at $320.
4. Under the video: "Earns commission", a button to a marketplace at more than 12x retail. She isn't the seller; she earns a cut.
5. The community explodes: where did this box come from?
6. You see: early creator boxes come through the official channel, with the source stated. This one has no source. Like showing off a film before it opens, with a link to black-market tickets beside it.
7. Her answer: "I chose to spend my own money on this." No proof she stole it or knew. But eBay pulls 30th listings: no stolen goods. The video is deleted.
8. Nobody proved the box was stolen, but everyone saw the button.

## Fairness line
She is never shown stealing, hiding anything or being accused by the film itself. The box's origin is a **question mark**, never an answer. No likeness: a generic creator figure. No Pokémon Company, YouTube, GOAT or eBay logos: generic screens with plain text ("Shop", "Earns commission", "Listing removed").

## Hero objects
- **The Night UPC box:** a built box (deep night blue, gold trim, a big window panel with the real Umbreon scan as the art, "30" in gold), in shrink-wrap, with a small sticker `RELEASE NOV 6`. Opened in S01: the lid comes off and stays off, beside the box, for the rest of her room shots.
- **The phone with the Short:** a viewer's phone, always seen from the holder's side (POV over the shoulder): her video playing, and under it a red **Shop · $2,262** button and the small grey line `Earns commission`. The button is the reel's recurring image.
- **The Magikarp:** the real Paldea Evolved Magikarp illustration rare scan (sv2 203), held up to her camera; a hand-written sticky note `$320` beside it.

The camera pushes **through the phone screen** into her bright creator room (travel), and on "nút mua" (f123) a giant red Shop button sign drops into frame on two wires in front of her counter and swings to rest with `$2,262`. Title `$2,200`.
- **S05 "hơn mười hai lần":** the button leads through to a marketplace counter: one box on the left with the shop tag `$179.99`; on the right, boxes drop one by one into a tower (physics bake, each lands and settles) as the VO counts, ending 12 tall, filling the vertical frame (tilt up). Title `x12`.
- **S11 analogy:** a cinema front with a big `COMING SOON` poster and a closed door; a man stands at the poster with a film reel tin held up proudly, and beside him a little stand with a hand sign `TICKETS` and a link-chain icon; on "chợ đen" a shady seller behind it flips the sign to `x12`.

## Sets and journeys
1. **Viewer's sidewalk / bus stop** (sunny street, trees, shop fronts, blue sky with clouds): the viewer stands at the bus stop shelter holding his phone. Home of the POV phone shots (S01, S15, S17).
2. **Her creator room** (bright: pastel-pink and cream walls, a ring light, shelves packed with sealed product boxes and plushies, a poster wall, a big window with blue sky, a wall calendar on SEPTEMBER, a standing-height filming counter with a camera on a tripod; she stands, never sits). S01-S04, S06, S08, S10, S12.
3. **The marketplace counter** (a glossy sneaker-store-like shop, white and gold, spotlights, a long counter, the seller behind it in a hoodie and cap; generic, no brand). S05-S06.
4. **The town plaza** (fountain, café fronts, string flags): the community: a crowd of 6-8 figures with phones. S07.
5. **The loading dock** (the "you see" set: a bright warehouse dock with stacked cartons, a roll-up door, a courier with a hand truck): an official sample carton with a big label `FROM: OFFICIAL / TO: CREATOR`. S09.
6. **The cinema front** (red-and-white striped awning, bulb-lit marquee, poster cases). S11.
7. **The listings board** (a cork notice board in the plaza covered with printed listings of 30th boxes): S14.
- Route: bus stop → through the phone into her room → glide out through the Shop button to the marketplace → dissolve to the plaza → cut back to her room (Khoan) → dissolve to the dock → dissolve to her room (the box, no label) → dissolve to the cinema → her room (her reply) → plaza board (eBay) → cut to the bus stop phone (deleted) → pull back to the whole street.

## Cast
- **The creator** (generic, no likeness): blonde ponytail, pink hoodie, light jeans.
- **The viewer** (bus stop): teal tee, grey cap, backpack. Same figure in S01, S15, S17.
- **Marketplace seller:** black hoodie, gold chain, cap; behind the counter only.
- **Crowd** in the plaza: 6-8 generic figures in bright tees, phones in hand.
- **Courier** (brown uniform, cap) at the dock; **cinema man** (red jacket) and **ticket tout** (trench coat, sunglasses).

## Visual laws
- **The button is the hook and the ending.** Red, rounded, `Shop · $2,262`; it appears in S01, S05, S15 and S17 and never moves on the screen.
- **The box's origin is a label.** The official carton has a full shipping label; her box has none: a bare spot where a label would go (S10).
- **Her room never darkens.** The tone is a question, not a crime scene: the light stays bright; only the crowd and the board carry the heat.

## Gags (literal)
- "hai tháng trước ngày bán": the calendar on her wall reads SEPTEMBER; the box sticker reads NOV 6; the camera pans from one to the other.
- "dòng chữ nhỏ": a magnifier-style push in on the tiny grey `Earns commission` line under the button (on the phone, POV).
- "cô nhận hoa hồng": at the marketplace, a gold coin slides from the till along the counter and drops into a little jar beside her labelled `COMMISSION` (one motion, one coin, plink).
- "Cộng đồng nổ tung": in the plaza every phone buzzes at once (a ring of shake lines), heads snap down to the screens, then up, jaws open (`gape`).
- "Khoan": hard cut, everything freezes; the box alone on her counter, the camera circles it once.
- "không ai nói nó đến từ đâu": her box turned to show the bare spot where a label would be; a single question-mark sticker.
- "Chưa có bằng chứng": a detective-style cork board on her room wall with just one pushpin and nothing on it (one-shot sign gag).
- "eBay đã gỡ": on the plaza board, listings flip over one by one to a red `REMOVED` back.
- "Video đã bị xóa": hard cut back to the bus-stop phone: the video area goes grey with `Video unavailable`; the button area under it is now empty.
- "ai cũng đã kịp thấy": pull back down the street: at the bus stop, in the café, on the bench-free plaza, every phone in every hand shows the same red Shop button (screenshots), and the camera shutter meme clicks.

## Energy and light
Bright sunny day throughout; her room warm and bright. Builds from the hook to the 12-box tower (S05), peaks in the plaza (S07), drops to a dead-still freeze on "Khoan" (S08), calm and clean for the dock (S09), playful at the cinema (S11), level and fair for her answer and the no-proof beats, ends on a wide sunny street with every phone glowing.
