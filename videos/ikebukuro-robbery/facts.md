# Ikebukuro card shop robbery: facts
Sources:
- https://en.friday.news/article/47539 (tabloid-style)
- Yomiuri text via https://toushichannel.net/archives/46750732.html
- NHK, Yahoo Japan and Asahi pages returned 404 and were seen only as search snippets.

## Confirmed (allegations; arrests on suspicion)
- About 8:30 p.m. on 31 Dec 2025, at a card shop in Higashi-Ikebukuro, Toshima Ward, Tokyo.
- Suspects posed as customers, grabbed the owner (in his 30s) and smashed the display case with a hammer(-like tool).
- 14 Pokémon cards worth about 26.9 million yen (roughly $180,000) were taken and have not been recovered.
- The Tokyo Metropolitan Police announced on 28 Oct 2026 the arrest of four men aged 24 to 44, on suspicion of robbery and trespass.
- One reportedly said he applied to a "dark part-time job" advertised on social media (Yomiuri). Police suspect accomplices or a ringleader.
