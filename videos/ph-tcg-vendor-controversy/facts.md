# Philippine Pokémon TCG vendor controversy: facts
Sources:
- https://technology.inquirer.net/150410/recapping-the-recent-pokemon-tcg-ph-controversy (Doby Rodriguez, 14 Nov 2026)
- https://technology.inquirer.net/150635/charess-stands-her-ground-cosplay-is-not-consent (25 Nov 2026)

**Single outlet (Inquirer).** Every allegation is unproven, and nothing has been legally established.

## Confirmed (as Inquirer reports it)
- A Pokémon TCG Philippines subreddit thread warned about a known seller. Leaked group-chat messages allegedly showed inappropriate conduct, including toward a woman and a minor.
- MNL Card Convention "removed [him] as a vendor and sponsor from its event".
- Some people in the same vendor circle defended him; critics pointed to ties within that circle and called for boycotts.
- Another vendor allegedly sought to trace the IP addresses of anonymous Reddit users. There's no confirmation any tracing happened.
- A US TCG creator shared that vendor's Reddit apology and was threatened with legal action.
- Both vendors eventually apologised; their wording isn't reported.
- Inquirer raises Safe Spaces Act (RA 11313) questions and recommends:
  - conduct standards where young people attend;
  - easy reporting channels;
  - protection from retaliation.
