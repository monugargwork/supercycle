# Mira Mesa Bubble Mew theft: facts
Sources (the CBS8 article named in the topic returned 403 and could not be read):
- https://www.10news.com/news/local-news/pokemon-card-stolen-from-girl-at-her-first-collectibles-show-in-mira-mesa
- https://www.10news.com/news/local-news/rare-pokemon-card-stolen-12-year-old-girl-still-hoping-for-answers

## Confirmed (10News)
- A 12-year-old girl's first collectibles show with her father, at Mira Mesa High School, San Diego.
- A man took one of the most valuable cards in their booth out of the father's hand the moment he handed it to him. The card is "a rare Bubble Mew", valued at "over $3,000" by the father.
- Father's quotes: "It happened to be one of the pricier ones in the booth. The moment I handed it to him... it was gone." "I thought he was joking, I thought he was gonna take two steps and run back."
- He tried to chase but couldn't, because of a broken heel, and hurt it more.
- Girl's quote: "I just want to make sure it doesn't happen to anyone else."
- San Diego police are investigating, with several leads and no arrest. They released photos of what they believe is the suspect's black sedan.
- Organizers added security guards and vendor rules. Someone gave the father a replica of the card, and a GoFundMe is raising about a third of the card's value.

## The card (added at kickoff, 27 Nov 2026)
- "Bubble Mew" is the collectors' name for the Paldean Fates Mew ex 232/091 special illustration rare (Mew asleep inside a bubble, illus. USGMEN). Real scan: `library/cards/sv4pt5/bubble_mew.png` (pokemontcg.io, 733x1024).
- The reports don't say whether it was graded. On screen it sits in a clear magnetic one-touch case (no slab label, so no invented grade or cert), which keeps the real card face large and readable.
- The girl also collided with the running thief and was bruised (second 10News report). Not in the VO, so not shown.
