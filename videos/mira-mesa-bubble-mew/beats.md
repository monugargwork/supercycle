# Mira Mesa (Bubble Mew): beats

VO: `voice.mp3` (82.5 s, ends f2476). Reel 2510 f. Script as approved in `script.md`; facts in `facts.md`.

**Logline:** at their very first card show, in a San Diego high-school gym, a father hands his 12-year-old daughter's prized Bubble Mew to a stranger who asked to look, and the stranger simply runs off with it while the father, limping on a bad heel, can't catch him; the police chase a black sedan, the community chips in, and the girl turns the loss into a rule that protects every other seller.

## Spine
1. A man is holding a $3,000+ card, then he's gone, at a father and daughter's first show (hook).
2. Their first booth: Mira Mesa High gym, the Bubble Mew as the star of the table.
3. The father hands it to a stranger to look; it vanishes from his hand. He thought it was a joke, two steps and back. It wasn't.
4. He chases, limping on an already-hurt heel, and hurts it more.
5. Why run for one card? You see: handing over a card is as normal as handing a stranger your phone for a photo; everyone watches the camera, nobody watches the holder. Cheap trust, fast loss.
6. Police release the black sedan photo; no arrest. Organizers add guards and vendor rules; a replica and a fundraiser.
7. The thief was fast; the girl looks further: her rule now stands on every table.

## Hero objects
- **The Bubble Mew** (real Paldean Fates 232/091 scan) in a clear magnetic one-touch case, on a little wooden easel at the front of the booth with a price tag `$3,200`. After the theft the easel stands **empty** for the rest of the reel, with the tag still clipped to it.
- **The booth table:** standing table with a sky-blue cloth, a glass-top display case of top-loaders (real library scans), a hand-lettered cardboard sign `FIRST SHOW!` and a cash tin.
- **The gym doors** (double doors with push bars, a bright parking lot behind): where he runs, where he doesn't come back, where the guard stands at the end.

## Hero moments ★
- **S01 hook:** the stranger holds the case up to the light, admiring; the father's hand is still open where he passed it. On "chạy mất" (f103) the stranger bolts out of frame with speed lines; a stack of top-loaders spins off the table edge and clatters to the floor (physics), and the camera whips after him toward the gym doors.
- **S05 "biến mất khỏi tay ông":** close two-shot of the hand-over (the card fills ~45% of the frame for 1.5 s before), then on "biến" the stranger is gone in one frame; the father's hand stays open on empty air, the price tag swings on the empty easel. Hard cut (punch).
- **S07-S08 the chase:** the father sets off, one leg stiff, a hop-limp across the court lines; on "đau" a red pain star pops at his heel, and on "đau thêm" he stops dead, lifts the bad leg and winces (no kneeling or falling); far ahead the stranger bursts through the doors and a black sedan pulls out of the lot.

## Sets and journeys
1. **The gym (Mira Mesa High card show):** warm maple floor with painted court lines and the school logo at centre court, folded bleachers on one wall with a `MIRA MESA` banner, a scoreboard, a CARD SHOW banner, two rows of vendor tables (sellers standing behind them), shoppers browsing, high windows with sky. Their booth near the double doors.
2. **Outside the gym:** the school front (cream stucco, red trim, palm trees), the parking lot with painted bays, a few cars, the black sedan, blue sky with clouds. Reached by the camera flying through the open doors.
3. **The beach overlook (the "you see" set):** a sunny San Diego seaside promenade, rail, palms, the pier and ocean behind, blue sky: a family hands their phone to a stranger for a photo.
- Route: gym (hook, booth, hand-over) → through the doors to the lot (the chase) → back in → dissolve to the beach (analogy) → dissolve back to the booth's empty easel → the gym's notice board (police flyer) → the doors (new guard) → the booth (replica, fundraiser jar, her sign) → crane up over the whole gym.

## Cast
- **Father** (generic, no likeness): navy-and-white baseball tee, red cap, glasses, khaki shorts. The limp is acted (stiff leg, hop step, wince) since the figures have no feet.
- **Daughter, 12** (kid-size card): yellow hoodie, two pigtails with red ties.
- **Stranger/thief:** tall, maroon hoodie with the hood down, blue jeans, a cap; never described by police, so nothing more.
- **Shoppers and vendors:** 4-6 generic figures in bright tees (teal, orange, green, red).
- **Officer** (light-blue shirt, badge) in S12; **security guard** (yellow SECURITY vest) at the doors in S13.
- **Beach family** (mum, dad, kid) and a **beach stranger** with sunglasses in the analogy.

## Visual laws
- **Trust is the open hand.** Handing over = an open palm; the father's empty open hand after the theft is the image of the loss. In the analogy the phone passes from open hand to open hand.
- **The loss stays:** the empty easel with its price tag, in every booth shot after S05.
- **Attention is eye-line:** in the analogy, every eye is on the phone's lens; nobody's eyes are on the holder (shown from the phone's POV, then wide).

## Gags (literal)
- "chạy hai bước rồi quay lại": the stranger takes two playful backward steps (the father laughs, waiting for him to come back), then turns and sprints.
- "Anh ta không quay lại": a held, locked shot of the empty double doors swinging shut and settling (the same framing as the run-out), a beat of stillness.
- "ai cũng nhìn vào máy, chẳng ai nhìn người cầm": phone POV of the smiling family; reverse wide: the family frozen in their pose, every eye on the lens, nobody looking at the man holding the phone.
- "Kẻ trộm chạy rất nhanh": a tiny far figure sprinting across the lot (callback).
- "người nhìn xa hơn": crane up from her booth to the whole gym: every vendor table now has her sign.

## Energy and light
Bright midday throughout (high gym windows, sun outside). Builds from the hook to the vanish (S05), peaks at the chase (S07-S08), one beat of stillness on the empty doors (S06b) and on "Khoan" (S09). Calm, warm and breezy for the beach (S10-S11). Steady for the aftermath, uplifting at the end.
