# MTG binder at Vectis: facts
Source: https://www.thenorthernecho.co.uk/news/26751879.rare-magic-gathering-cards-turn-60-lot-14-700-sale/ (Andrew White, 23 Nov 2026), read in full. The Vectis site's own headline says "A hidden Magic: The Gathering discovery sells for £14,700!"

## Confirmed (Northern Echo)
- **The lot:** a Magic: The Gathering collection stored for years in the vendor's loft after he stopped playing. He consigned his full collection to Vectis Auctions, Thornaby.
- **Estimate:** £40-£60. Inside a "fairly ordinary green ring binder", among plastic sleeves, were three cards:
  - The Tabernacle at Pendrell Vale (Legends);
  - Candelabra of Tawnos (Antiquities);
  - Mishra's Workshop (Antiquities).
- **Before the sale:** news spread among collectors, and pre-auction bidding reached £4,800.
- **The sale (11 Nov 2026):** bidding opened at £7,000 and the hammer fell at £12,000. With premium the total was £14,700, more than 240x the top estimate. Lot 196.
- **Jake Kenyon (Vectis specialist):** the cards are valuable "not only for their age, but also because these are competitive cards, so competitive that they used to be banned or limited in official tournaments". Collectors "asked for many scans and pictures"; "the bidding was ferocious".

## Card background (other sources)
- Tabernacle and Mishra's Workshop are on the Reserved List (never reprinted). Tabernacle: CGC has certified 69 copies.
