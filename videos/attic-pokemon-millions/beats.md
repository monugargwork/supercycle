# Attic Pokémon collection: beat sheet (stage 1)

VO: voice.mp3 (77.1 s, 2313 f at 30 fps). Words: words.json (aligned to script.md, match 0.88).
Build: the house 2D cards with the GPS-v2 improvements (arms that really reach props, crouch/bend to reach, fixed-length

## Logline
A retired British painter buys his son's playground craze "just in case", files it away untouched in the attic,
forgets it for fifteen years, and walks out of an auction house £1.5 million richer.

## Spine
1. He thinks the cards look nice and might be worth something, so he buys complete sets.
2. His son gets bored; he keeps buying and stores every card untouched.
3. The binders go up into the attic and are forgotten for fifteen years.
4. A newspaper says one card sold for £6,000: he goes back up.
5. At Ewbank's, complete and untouched turns out to be very rare.
6. £300 becomes almost £100,000; the whole collection £1.5m. Treasure in the attic, literally.

## Hero objects
- **The binders:** chunky ring albums (navy, red, green), 30 x 32 x 6 cm, clear 9-pocket pages, every pocket full.
  The Skyridge one is green with a white label strip. They travel: desk -> attic shelf -> auction counter -> rostrum.
- **The attic hatch:** a ceiling panel with a pull-cord and a fold-down wooden ladder (3 hinged sections). It opens, slams, collects dust.
- **Ewbank's price board:** a flip/LED board behind the rostrum that climbs from £300 to £97,500, then to £1,560,000.

## Hero moments ★
- **S01 hook:** he shoves the binders into the attic, the hatch slams on "quên", dust falls on him, then fifteen years pile up in one shot.
- **S11 jaws drop:** the auction room's jaws literally drop to the floor (from the real "jaws hit the floor").
- **S18-S19 the board:** £300 rolls to £97,500 (gavel on "bảng"), then on to £1.56m with the room erupting.

## Sets and journeys
1. **Painter's house:** hallway with the attic hatch + ladder; the attic above (rafters, insulation, a round window, boxes);
   his study with the desk and computer. Same study framing used twice: late 90s (beige CRT) and 15 years later (flat screen, older him).
2. **School playground, late 90s:** a fence, a bench, kids trading cards.
3. **Ewbank's auction room:** rows of chairs, rostrum with gavel, the price board, an "EWBANK'S" sign on the wall.
4. **"You see" table:** a warm top-down macro on a wooden table for the explanation (loose cards vs his binder).
- Journeys: attic -> (dissolve back in time) playground -> fence to his study (travel) -> attic (crane up through the hatch)
  -> (dissolve 15 years) study -> ladder -> auction room (travel through the doors) -> table macro -> playground gags -> study -> auction -> attic.

## Cast
- **Painter (him):** white paint-splattered overalls, flat cap, grey moustache; older in the "15 years later" half (whiter hair, cardigan over the overalls).
  Items: binders, a mouse, a newspaper, a teacup. Views: front, side.
- **Son (kid, late 90s):** red cap, backpack, striped T-shirt. Items: loose cards. Then just a kid at the shelf.
- **School friends (2):** trading cards, the doubters' chorus in S05.
- **Auctioneer:** suit and bow tie, gavel. **Ewbank's valuer:** cardigan, glasses (the jaw-drop and the skeptic).
- **Bidders chorus (5-6):** the room that reacts (jaws drop, cheer).

## Visual laws
- **Untouched = shine:** his binders and cards always carry a clean gleam sweep; kids' cards are bent, scuffed, never shine.
- **Time = dust and the computer:** dust and cobwebs pile up in the attic; the study's beige CRT becomes a flat screen and his hair whitens.
- **Value = the price board** in Ewbank's, never floating numbers. Key numbers also go in the house titles.

## Gags (literal sight gags)
- "cả phòng há hốc miệng" -> everyone's jaw drops all the way to the floor (Instagram thud).
- "Người lớn mà đi sưu tầm thẻ của trẻ con?" -> the son and his friends stare at him from the doorway, deadpan.
- "Ông thì vẫn mua." -> the parcel stack is now taller than him.
- "chưa bao giờ được đụng vào" -> the son's hand creeps toward a binder; dad swats it away (slap).
- "kho báu ... nghĩa đen" -> he opens the attic hatch and gold light floods out over the binders.

## Energy map, light
- Calm, warm hook -> playful middle (playground, eBay clicks) -> stillness before the reveal (S08 dust, the one quiet beat)
  -> climb (S09-S10) -> climax S11 and S18-S19 (auction, bright) -> warm golden coda in the attic.
- Light: attic warm dusty beams; playground bright day; study day (90s) / soft evening (15 years later); auction bright interior; coda golden.
