# Attic Pokémon collection: facts

## Sources
- Kotaku (John Walker, 22 Oct 2026): https://kotaku.com/pokemon-tcg-auction-skyridge-master-set-base-aquapolis-millionaire-2000748794
- Nintendo Life: https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/10/we-couldnt-believe-it-mans-forgotten-pokemon-collection-sells-for-usd2-million
- Coverage also in BBC (cited), LBC, ThePrint, IBTimes.

## Confirmed
- Seller: retired painter and decorator from Woking, UK; anonymous. (Kotaku)
- Late 1990s his son traded cards at school; he "thought they looked nice" and "wondered whether they might be a good investment". (Kotaku, quote)
- Bought complete sets on eBay (from a Malaysian seller, per Nintendo Life); kept collecting after his son lost interest.
- Son "had his own cards... but he never got to touch these, I'm glad to say." (quote)
- Binders went into the loft about 15 years ago and were forgotten; a newspaper report of a single card selling for over £6,000 sent him to the loft.
- Ewbank's auction "Pokemon: A Decade of Masters 1999-2009" (21 Oct 2026): total £1.56m (~$2.09m); Ewbank's most successful sale in 30 years; "jaws hit the floor" when he brought it in.
- Skyridge full set: paid £300, sold £97,500 incl. fees (£75,000 hammer per Nintendo Life).
- Legendary Collection master set: est. £15–20k, sold £130,000 (£100,000 hammer). Aquapolis master set: £49,500.
- Also EX Team Rocket, EX Delta Species, EX Deoxys, a Pop Series binder.
- Seller quote: "I know people always talk about finding treasure in the attic, but in my case it's literally true."
- Andrew Ewbank: "extensive, untouched, complete, and with just about every rarity you could hope for."
