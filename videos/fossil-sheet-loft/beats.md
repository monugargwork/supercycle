# Fossil sheet in the loft: beat sheet (stage 1)

VO: `voice.mp3` → `voice.wav` (Trung Caha, 75.8 s, **2275 f** at 30 fps; the reel runs 2290 f). Words: `words.json`, aligned to `script.md`. Every `check:` line was a number or name whisper wrote differently (27, 90, 6, 10.000, 29, 11, Pokemon, Collins/Cullens, chảy/trầy, dấy/giấy).
Composition `FossilReel`, code `src/videos/fossil/`.

## Logline
A 1999 uncut Fossil holo sheet, rolled up and forgotten in a loft for 27 years next to a broken fan, is nearly binned as old wallpaper, until it turns out to be a KB Toys promo sheet straight off the press, now photographed like a film star for a £6-10k auction. So maybe check the fan too.

## Spine
1. 1999: someone rolls an uncut sheet and shoves it into the loft. 27 years pass: dust, spiders, the broken fan, nobody touches it.
2. 2026 clear-out: it's pulled out, unrolled, and it's a wreck (scratches, glue, creases, a torn back). "Wallpaper? Bin it."
3. The owner sees the world going mad for Pokémon, saves it from the bin, and takes it to be valued, lottery-face on.
4. Wait, why is there an uncut sheet in a house? A relative worked at KB Toys in the 90s.
5. It's a Fossil holo sheet straight off the press, before it's chopped into cards, printed for a KB Toys promotion.
6. Now it's shot like a star with a condition report: Cullens of Surrey, £6,000-£10,000, Sunday 29 Nov.
7. And the broken fan is still in the loft. Who knows.

## Hero objects
- **The sheet:** a built 3D uncut sheet, about 60 x 85 cm, a grid of the REAL Fossil holo scans (11 columns x 11 rows, the 15 holos repeating in print order; the thin white gutters and trim marks of a press sheet). It's a curled sheet (a bent mesh) that rolls into a tube about 9 cm across, held by a rubber band. Damage layers that stay on in every later shot: scratches, glue blobs, crease lines, and a torn flap on the back corner. The holo foil gets a moving gleam.
- **The broken fan:** a 1990s standing fan, about 1.1 m, a cage with one bent bar, one blade hanging loose, the cord knotted round its neck. It sits beside the tube in 1999 and is still there at the end.
- **The loft hatch + ladder:** a square hatch in the landing ceiling, a hinged door that swings down, a folding wooden ladder. The way in and out of the loft in every era.

## Hero moments ★
- **S01, the hook:** 1999, the relative (red KB Toys staff vest) on the ladder, his head and arms up in the hatch. He snaps the rubber band round the rolled sheet and shoves it into the dark on "nhét" (f67). It rolls, bumps the fan (the loose blade swings) and stops. He climbs down, and the hatch slams shut on "quên" (f122): a dust puff falls from the seams; the camera starts its push up through the gap into the loft while the dust is still falling.
- **S07-S09, the wreck and "wallpaper":** the owner unrolls the sheet on the landing floor. It springs back half-curled, so she weights the corners with a mug, a shoe and a paint tin. A macro crawls the damage on each word (scratch f653, glue f687, crease f716; the corner flips, torn back f767). Then the partner holds it flat against the wall: it looks exactly like the old wallpaper (same faded grid). "Vứt đi" (f834): he swings it toward an open bin bag.
- **S13, off the press and chopped:** through the sheet into a print shop. A fresh, perfect twin sheet slides out of the press rollers (f1585). A guillotine blade drops on "chặt" (f1618), and a fan of single cards (real scans) spills and skids across the table and comes to rest.

## Sets and journeys
1. **Loft:** under a pitched roof (rafters, felt, insulation between joists), boards laid over the joists, the hatch in the floor. Lived-in junk: the fan, a suitcase, a lampshade, a box of Christmas lights, a rusty pair of scissors on a nail, an empty picture frame leaning on a rafter, a calendar nailed to a rafter. One small gable window: dim blue-grey light with a dusty light shaft. Ceiling = the roof, so no sky.
2. **Landing** (below the hatch): a narrow upstairs hall with faded grid wallpaper, carpet, a radiator, a bin bag, a mug, a shoe and a paint tin from the clear-out, a cardboard box marked "KHO". A TV in the next room is seen through a door frame.
3. **Auction house (Cullens):** one interior set in three corners: the valuation counter (green mat, lamp, loupe), a photo corner (easel, two softboxes, a camera on a tripod), and the saleroom (rostrum, lot board, a few chairs). Day light, warm practicals.
4. **KB Toys store, 1999 (flashback, golden):** shelves of toy boxes, a counter with a till, a promotion board with an uncut sheet pinned on it, a hanging "KB TOYS" sign (plain lettering, no logo).
5. **Print shop, 1999 (flashback, golden-cool):** an offset press with rollers and an outfeed tray, a guillotine cutter on a table.
- Route: landing 1999 (hook) → push up through the hatch into the loft → locked loft frame (27 years) → hatch bangs open, 2026 → down the ladder to the landing → dissolve to the counter → (cut) valuer's "khoan đã" → dissolve to the 1999 store → dissolve back to the counter → dolly into the holo gleam → through the sheet into the print shop → dissolve to the store promo board → dissolve to the photo corner → glide to the saleroom → dissolve to the loft.

## Visual laws
- **Time = dust and webs.** In the locked loft frame the dust layer on the tube thickens and the cobweb between the fan and the tube grows strand by strand. Time prop: the 1999 calendar on the rafter, its pages flipping on their own to 2026.
- **Worth = light on the foil.** Damaged and dull in the loft and on the landing (no gleam); from "hologram" on, a gleam sweeps the foil; in the photo corner it's flashes and softboxes.
- **"Nobody" = three things left undone,** in the same locked frame: rusty scissors that never moved (cut), an empty frame that stays empty (frame), and the web that seals the tube over (remember).

## Gags
- "lâu hơn tuổi đời của nhiều người đang xem" → the calendar flips through the years (the 1999 page is the first; title 1999 → 2026 only on the next line).
- "cái quạt hỏng mà không ai dám vứt" → in 2003 / 2011 / 2019 (calendar), a hand-less arm reaches up through the hatch, touches the fan, the loose blade swings, the arm pulls back down. Three times.
- "Giấy dán tường hả?" → he holds the sheet against the wall, and it matches the wallpaper.
- "với vẻ mặt của người vừa dò vé số" → at the counter she squeezes her eyes shut and peeks with one, sweat drop; a lottery ticket is tucked in her cardigan pocket.
- "chụp cận từng vết xước như minh tinh" → a red rope on stands in front of the easel, flashes popping on every scratch the camera zooms to.
- "Còn cái quạt hỏng... Biết đâu đấy." → the partner, back up the ladder, eyes the fan; the loose blade finally drops off (clank on "gác", f2240); he squints at it.
