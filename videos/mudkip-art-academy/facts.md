## Confirmed
- Card: 2015 Pokémon Japanese XY Promo Art Academy Daisuke Ochiai Mudkip #XY-P, PSA 10. Non-holo, event stamp.
- Origin: one of 10 winning designs from the first Pokémon Art Academy contest in Japan (Polkastarter quoting Goldin). Art Academy = Nintendo 3DS drawing-lesson game; in the EU/NA contest entries were drawn in the game and posted to Miiverse (Serebii). Japan entry method not confirmed, so the script says "a Pokémon drawing game" only.
- Prize: each winning artist received 100 copies of their card (Goldin lot text; Serebii says the same for EU/NA; Pokumon est. 100). Never sold at retail.
- Goldin Pokémon 30th Anniversary Auction, closed 25–26 Oct 2026: $4.0M hammer, $4.88M with 22% premium, 73 bids. PSA 10 pop 3 (Goldin title). Auction total over $28M, 18 records.
- Price history (PSA 10): $42,000 at Fanatics, 21 Nov 2024 (Polkastarter only); $3.72M at Fanatics Premier, 25 Sept 2026 (SI, Polkastarter).
- Raincoat Pikachu (2015 Japanese XY Promo Art Academy, Y. Fujishima, from the global 3DS contest), PSA 10: $8.4M at Fanatics on 28 Sept 2026, 64 bids (Toy-People). Three days after the $3.72M Mudkip.
- Same Goldin auction: 1997 Gold 1st Place Trophy Pikachu PSA 8, $3,080,500, 32 bids, second-highest result (SI weekly top five).
- Mudkip = the Mud Fish Pokémon ("cá bùn").

## Worth showing
- The 3DS-style drawing game, a stylus drawing a Mudkip; a stack of exactly 100 printed cards handed to the winner; PSA slab with "10"; auction paddles; the Trophy Pikachu (gold card) coming second; Raincoat Pikachu (red raincoat, umbrella).
