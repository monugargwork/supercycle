# Mudkip Art Academy: beat sheet (stage 1)

VO: voice.wav (Trung Caha, 74.6 s, 2238 f at 30 fps; the reel runs 2250 f). Words: words.json (aligned to script.md; the take matches the script word for word).
Build: the house flat cartoon cards in the inked 3D world, real card scans everywhere a card shows (cards/SOURCES.txt), burned-in captions, meme sounds only where they fit.

## Logline
A drawing a fan made in a Nintendo drawing game became a prize card of 100 copies; a decade later one copy outsold the most famous trophy card in the hobby, because only three perfect ones exist.

## Spine
1. Someone pays nearly $5 million for a Mudkip card.
2. Collectors laugh: it is not even a Charizard.
3. It was never a factory card: one fan drew it for a contest, and it won.
4. The prize was his drawing as a card: 100 copies, all his. Never in shops or packs.
5. The price climbs: $42,000, then $3.72M, then a sister card at $8.4M.
6. One more Mudkip: 73 bids, $4.88M. More than a villa.
7. People are not paying for the Pokémon but for a number: 100 printed, 3 perfect.
8. The same night the legendary 1997 trophy Pikachu sells for $3M and comes second.

## Hero objects
- **The Mudkip slab:** the real 2015 Art Academy Mudkip scan in a PSA-style case, label GEM MT 10. It is on the cash desk, on the lot column, on the balance, on the table, on the top step.
- **The price board:** dark panel with amber digits behind the rostrum. It grows two wings when the price needs seven figures.
- **The 100:** first a block of cards in a parcel, later 100 cards spread on a table.

## Hero moments ★
- **S01 hook:** the buyer slams a sack of cash on the desk on "năm triệu"; bundles and bills fly; the camera leaves him for the little slab that the money bought.
- **S14 the hammer:** the gavel is held high while the board rolls to $4,880,000, a beat of stillness, then the strike.
- **S15 the villa:** a giant balance; he stands the slab on the near pan and the villa on the far pan shoots into the air.
- **S18-S19 the podium:** the trophy Pikachu's column sinks to step 2 beside the Mudkip on step 1; the laughing collectors' jaws hit the carpet.

## Sets and journeys
1. **Auction room:** stage with the rostrum, the lot column, the date plate and the price board; the cash desk by the entrance; the room where the collectors stand.
2. **Card factory:** an indexing conveyor and a print press: blanks in, cards out.
3. **Daisuke's room:** desk, the two-screen handheld, later the parcel with the 100 cards.
4. **Card shop:** counter and shelves of sealed boxes.
5. **The lawn:** a giant brass balance, a villa on the far pan.
6. **The table:** warm top-down macro for the explanation.
- Route: cash desk -> the room (dissolve) -> factory -> (dissolve, ten years back) his desk -> into the handheld's screen and out of the card (through-object) -> shop -> (dissolves for each date) the same locked frame on the stage four times -> the lawn (cut, a gag) -> the table -> the stage -> the room (cut, the reaction).

## Visual laws
- **Price = the board**, always in the same locked frame; **time = the flap date plate** above it (11.2024, 9.2026, three days later, a month later).
- **Scarcity = counting real cards on a table:** 100 dealt out, 3 get a case, 97 blown away.
- **Weight = money:** the sack squashes the desk; the slab outweighs the villa.
