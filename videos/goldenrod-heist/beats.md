## Logline
Four masked men smash their way into a Wisconsin Pokémon shop at 2 a.m. and clear out $310,000 before dawn: a perfect plan, except they came in a rented white minivan with their names on the paperwork, and the paper trail leads police straight to a house in a town called Justice.

## Spine
1. Hook: 2 a.m., a sledgehammer through the glass door, four masks, bolt cutters, a minivan at the curb.
2. Where: Goldenrod City Collectibles, Twin Lakes, Wisconsin. Four cases smashed, everything bagged, even the trailer out back.
3. Gone before the rooster: $310,000+. The perfect plan: masks, midnight, no faces.
4. Turn: the minivan is a rental, with a signed contract. A masked man doing paperwork.
5. You see: the mask hides a face, but the rental remembers every road and the phone every tower (hide-and-seek with a megaphone).
6. Proof: police pin camera, rental file, GPS, phone records and prints; the string runs to a house in Justice, Illinois, full of Goldenrod's stock.
7. Payoff: five charged, two arrested, three wanted, now on foot. Smashing glass takes seconds; the rental record stays for years.

## Visual laws
- **Memory = a glowing gold trail.** The rental's GPS draws the route as a gold line on the road behind the van; the phone lights each cell tower it touches (a blue ring, and the tower's top lamp stays on). The same gold route is on the GPS printout and the police map.
- **Identity = paper.** The rental agreement (signature, APPROVED stamp) is the hero object: signed at the counter, pinned on the board, filed in the archive at the end.
- Light: night blue for the heist and the drive; dawn gold when the rooster crows and for the raid in Justice; plain day for the offices.
