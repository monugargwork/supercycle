# Dodgeville Walmart "Spark driver" theft: facts
Sources:
- https://www.dexerto.com/pokemon/man-charged-after-allegedly-posing-as-walmart-delivery-driver-to-steal-pokemon-cards-3430116/ (20 Nov 2026)
- https://www.wmtv15news.com/2026/11/19/man-arrested-accused-posing-delivery-driver-stealing-pokmon-packs-dodgeville-walmart/

## Confirmed (all allegations)
- **The call:** police were called just after 11:15 a.m., Thursday 19 Nov 2026, for a theft in progress at the Dodgeville, Wisconsin Walmart.
- **The method:** police say he posed as a Spark delivery driver and stole large amounts of items during pickups. Spark is Walmart's app for drivers who collect and deliver orders. Neither article gives step-by-step detail.
- **The stop:** officers found a black Tesla hatchback with Florida plates and made a traffic stop.
- **Recovered:**
  - 125 packages of 30th Celebration boosters;
  - 112 One Piece packages;
  - 26 Pokémon 30th Celebration tins;
  - a Rayquaza Lego set;
  - about 69 golf balls, speakers, cameras and clothing.
- **Charge and links:** felony retail theft. Police say the Tesla had been to Madison, La Crosse, Fitchburg, Dubuque, Missouri and several Minnesota locations. They believe the items might be linked to thefts elsewhere and are asking other agencies to call.
