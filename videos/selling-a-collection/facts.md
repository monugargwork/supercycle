# Facts

Topic 7 of the November 2026 topics list (TCG_Story_Topics_with_Sources_November_2026.pdf). Script status: sent to the client for approval.

- Source: https://aftermath.site/pokemon-card-selling/ (Lucas DeRuyter, 26 Oct 2026), a first-person account.
- Three tubs of his fiancée's grandmother's Pokémon items; several dozen sealed packs (Base Set, Base Set 2, Jungle, Fossil, Rocket); ~$16,000. Collect-A-Con: 1.5 h in line, 8 business cards; YouTubers/streamers said yes then went silent. Late Sept an LA scalper/streamer replied; arrived 45 min late, weighed every pack for 2 h on the fiancée's jewelry scale; paid ~$17k by bank transfer, ~20% under market. Next Monday demanded money back (assumed Shadowless). Writer drove across LA with $3,500 cash, parking-lot buyback; sold to a shop that scanned his ID, doubted the cards were his, misspelled the check. Eight months total. Partner: "We shouldn't have done this." Writer: not worth any amount of money.
- Single source by nature (personal account).
