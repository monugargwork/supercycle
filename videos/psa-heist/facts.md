# PSA truck heist: facts

Taken from the approved script and the research for the first build (Oct–Nov 2026).

- 13 Oct 2026, Santa Ana, California: a FedEx truck parked outside PSA's grading facility.
- The driver left the truck; a thief removed the lock on the cargo box and took two pallets.
- The pallets held freshly graded cards on their way back to their owners.
- PSA's published list: 6,451 items stolen, close to 3,000 of them Pokémon.
- Police estimate the loss at close to US$1 million; the FBI is involved.
- Every PSA slab carries its own certification number, stored in PSA's database.
- Right after the theft PSA voided all those numbers and sent the list to marketplaces and auction houses.
- A voided number exposes the slab when someone tries to sell it; cracking the case removes the official grade.
- Private sales can still slip through, so buyers should always check the cert number.

Not known (don't show as fact): whether anyone was caught, whether cards were recovered.
