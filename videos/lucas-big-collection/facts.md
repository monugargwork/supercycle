# Lucas DeRuyter's fiancée's collection: facts (from the approved script)

- Lucas DeRuyter tried to sell his fiancée's childhood Pokémon collection: three plastic tubs, a few dozen sealed packs (Base Set, Jungle, Fossil, Rocket), about $16,000.
- Everyone said it would be easy. He queued 1.5 hours at a card show and messaged about a dozen video creators; all promised to buy, then went silent.
- End of September a Los Angeles dealer answered; he arrived 45 minutes late, weighed every pack on someone else's jewellery scale for two hours, then wired almost $17,000.
- The next Monday he asked for money back, having thought he bought rare Shadowless print.
- Lucas drove across LA with $3,500 cash in an envelope to a parking lot to buy back the cards, then took them to another shop, which scanned his ID, suspected stolen goods, and misspelt his name on the check.
- Eight months in all. Over pizza his fiancée said they shouldn't have done it; Lucas wrote no amount of money was worth it.
- Source: Lucas DeRuyter's own first-person account (the user's brief; the VO script is approved as written). Amounts are as he reported them.
