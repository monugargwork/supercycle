# Fanatics unpaid Pikachu: beat sheet (stage 1)

VO: voice.mp3 → voice.wav (Trung Caha, 77.1 s, 2312 f at 30 fps; the reel runs 2325 f). Words: words.json (aligned to script.md; the take matches the script word for word, with one breath, "Thì…", at 28.9 s).
Build: the house flat cartoon cards in the inked 3D world, real card scans wherever a card shows, burned-in captions, meme sounds only where the sound is the joke.

## Logline
An $8.4M raincoat Pikachu is recorded as paid, then vanishes from the sold list and comes back marked "cancelled". A news site finds millions of dollars of Pokémon sales quietly erased, after the whole market had already priced itself off them.

## Spine
1. A raincoat Pikachu sells for $8.4M and is reported as the second most expensive Pokémon card.
2. The record says PAID, then the record disappears, then it comes back marked CANCELLED.
3. It isn't only Pikachu: GempireCards finds millions in sales gone from the sold history ($108k, $336k…).
4. Fanatics: unpaid sales are removed "so the market information is accurate".
5. But prices work like house prices: one big sale lifts the whole street, and erasing it later doesn't bring the street back down.
6. No explanation yet. The Pikachu is still "No. 2 in the world", with a word in small print nobody reads: cancelled.

## Hero objects
- **The Pikachu slab:** the real 2015 Art Academy raincoat Pikachu scan (`pika_rain`, already in the library) in a PSA-style case, GEM MT 10 (`reuse:slabReal`). It's on the lot column, then in a museum case at the end.
- **The sold wall:** a big cork board on the auction hall's side wall, painted header "SOLD HISTORY". It holds rows of paper sale tickets (14 x 9 cm card stock), each with a real card thumbnail, a price and a status line, on brass push-pins. The Pikachu ticket is the big one in the middle. This is where the story of the records plays out, always in the same locked frame.
- **The price signs:** stake signs on a street of houses (the "you see" set). Price = how high a sign stands.

## Hero moments ★
- **S01 hook:** the gavel slams on "tám triệu tư" and the price board snaps to 8,400,000. The camera swings to the sold wall, where the Pikachu ticket's pin pops on "biến mất": the ticket slides down and drops into the gap behind the filing cabinet, leaving a pale clean rectangle on the cork.
- **S12-S13 the street:** a SOLD rider is slapped onto one house's sign, and the neighbours' signs shoot up on their stakes like a domino wave. Then the SOLD sign is yanked out of the lawn, but every other sign stays up high.
- **S15 the fine print:** the Pikachu sits in a museum case under a spotlight, with visitors snapping photos. The camera pushes past them, down the brass plaque, to a tiny word on its last line: CANCELLED.

## Sets and journeys

2. **Newsstand:** just outside the hall door. A rack of papers from several countries.
3. **The street:** five small houses on a gentle curve, lawns and stake signs, golden calm light. This is the "you see" set.
4. **Museum gallery:** dark room, one glass case on a plinth, spotlight, brass plaque.

## Visual laws
- **A sale's status = its ticket on the sold wall.** A green PAID stamp; gone (pale rectangle); back with a red CANCELLED stamp on its **back** (the "hidden data inside the page" is the side nobody sees).
- **Erased sales = tickets that evaporate into steam** ("bốc hơi", literally): curls of steam rise off the cork and leave bare pins. Later the pale outline fades too, "as if never sold".
- **Time = the wall calendar** beside the wall (9, 10, 11 · 2026), plus the date plate on stage.
- **Price = sign height** on the street.

## Gags
- "biến mất" → the ticket slips down behind the filing cabinet.
- "bốc hơi" → the tickets literally steam away.
- "để thông tin thị trường được chính xác" → the clerk lays a spirit level on the remaining tickets and the bubble centres.
- "cả khu phố lập tức nâng giá theo" → the neighbours' signs shoot up on their stakes, one after another.
- "Fanatics chưa giải thích" → the clerk walks into the office and shuts the door; the "CLOSED" tag on the knob swings.
- "kèm một chữ nhỏ không ai đọc" → every visitor faces the card; the camera alone reads the plaque's last line.

## Energy map, light
- Loud hook, then the auction (warm).
- The three sold-wall states get colder each month: October neutral, November blue-grey with a flickering tube light.
- The reveal of CANCELLED is the first stillness: 8 f of nothing before the stamp is seen.
- Gempire and the steam lift the pace; the clerk's level is a dry beat.
- The street is the golden calm "you see" set; the domino of signs is the loudest visual hit.
- The ending is dark, one spotlight, a slow push and a quiet last word.
