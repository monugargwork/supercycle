# Charleston Target line, father arrested: facts
Sources:
- https://kotaku.com/dad-has-6-year-old-daughter-save-his-spot-in-line-at-target-for-pokemon-cards-and-then-leaves-2000754745 (6 Nov 2026; mostly rewrites local reporting)

## Confirmed
- **When and where:** Friday 6 Nov 2026, the Target in West Ashley, Charleston, SC. Customers had camped overnight for a new Pokémon TCG release.
- **What happened:**
  - Sometime after 1 a.m. the father left his 6-year-old daughter with a pillow and blanket (witnesses, per local outlets).
  - Customers called police after about an hour (Kotaku: over an hour; one outlet: over 90 minutes). Police responded around 2:30 a.m. to a report of an "unattended child".
  - Police say he told detectives he went home and left his daughter to hold his place in line.
- **Outcome:** he was arrested and charged with "unlawful conduct toward a child". The girl was unharmed and placed in emergency protective custody with the SC Department of Social Services.
