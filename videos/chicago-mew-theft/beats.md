# Chicago (Old Skool TCG): beats

VO: `voice.mp3` (80.3 s, ends f2410). Reel 2440 f. Script as approved in `script.md`; facts in `facts.md`.

**Logline:** a man phones ahead, sells a shop owner some sealed Pokémon product for $300, and in the same visit lifts four cards worth $6,700 from the open box of new stock beside the register, right in front of the owner and under the cameras, leaving behind his face on the CCTV and his own phone number in the caller ID.

## Spine
1. He sells, gets paid, and steals from the same shop (hook).
2. The shop: two months old, busy on a Wednesday afternoon. He phoned ahead and walks in with sealed boxes.
3. The open box of new stock by the register: one card into his jacket, he sells, takes the cash, then three more.
4. The haul: Red RGB Mew $4,000, PSA 8 Scream Psyduck $1,800, the gold Mew and the M Charizard: $6,700.
5. It happened a few steps from the owner, under a camera anyone could see. Why?
6. You see: the owner watches the stock, not the guest who brings a gift (the house analogy, the spoons).
7. Trust has no camera; the shop does. Police get the number; he leaves with 4 cards and $300, and leaves his face and his number behind.

## Hero objects
- **The box of new stock** on the counter by the register: an open cardboard box with two rows of slabs and cases standing upright, each with a white shop price sticker. Every theft leaves a gap that stays (1 gap after S06, 4 after S08).
- **The counter phone** with a caller-ID screen showing his number `(773) 555-0142` (a fictional 555 number): set up in S04, paid off in S14, S19, S22.
- **The CCTV camera** in the ceiling corner (red REC lamp) and the **counter monitor** facing the customers (4 views, one of them the box).

## Hero moments ★
- **S01 hook:** three $100 bills slap into his palm on "ba / trăm / đô", and in the same breath his other hand slips a slab out of the box behind him; it vanishes into his jacket on "trộm".
- **S08-S09 the haul:** the jacket-lining insert with the four real cards (real slab photos and the real Red RGB Mew scan), each with its shop sticker, then all four for the $6,700 title.
- **S19 "Tiệm thì có":** the warm handshake (trust) hard-cuts to the CCTV lens with its red REC lamp.

## Sets and journeys
1. **Shop interior** (Old Skool TCG): glass counter across the room, register, the box, phone; behind it, a wall of shelves full of sealed product, posters, a wall clock; a locked glass case on the left wall; a play table with binders; a ceiling camera and a customer monitor. Bright warm wood, cream walls, red-and-white accents; the front window shows the street and a blue sky.
2. **Street** (N. Milwaukee Ave, Chicago): a red-brick two-storey storefront with the OLD SKOOL TCG sign and a GRAND OPENING banner, neighbouring shops, a rooftop water tower, a far skyline, sidewalk with a lamp post, hydrant and a planter tree; blue sky with clouds.
3. **The house** (the analogy): a warm dining room with a front door, a table with a spoon tray, a cloudy-sky window.
4. **The jacket lining** (insert): the inside of his camel jacket, four clear pockets holding the four cards.
- Route: street → through the shop window into the shop (travel) → inside, the camera glides between counter, box, register and the ceiling camera → the jacket (push into the pocket, through-object dissolve) and back → dissolve to the house and back → the street for the exit.

## Cast
- **Owner** (generic; no likeness of Nick Bruni): green OLD SKOOL staff tee, red cap, glasses. Items: cash, phone handset.
- **Seller/thief:** camel jacket (the pockets matter), blue jeans, dark hair, no disguise. Items: a stack of three sealed booster boxes, then cash; his own phone at the end.
- **Customers** (busy shop): kid in a yellow hoodie, teen in a sky-blue tee, woman in a red jacket, man in a teal jacket.
- **Officer:** light-blue shirt, blue trousers, a checkered cap band, gold badge, a notepad.
- In the analogy the owner plays the host and the seller the guest, carrying one of his booster boxes gift-wrapped with a bow.

## Visual laws
- **Every theft leaves a gap** in the box that stays for the rest of the reel. In the house, the spoon tray gets the same gap.
- **The owner's attention is his eye-line:** he looks hard at hands on the merchandise (the kid at the case) and smiles at the man who sells to him.
- **Being recorded is the red REC lamp,** shown on the camera and on the monitor.

## Gags (literal)
- "đứng ngay đó": he doesn't move a step; only his far arm goes back into the box.
- "khách đến chơi nhà mang theo quà": the seller at a front door with a gift-wrapped booster box.
- "đếm thìa": the host turns his back to fetch cake; the spoon tray by the guest ends with one slot empty, rhyming with the box.
- "nó không có camera / Tiệm thì có": the handshake, then a hard cut to the camera lens.
- Ending: on "số điện thoại của chính mình" his own phone rings in his pocket on the sidewalk; he freezes. (No arrest is reported, so nobody arrests him.)

## Energy and light
Bright day throughout. Builds through the thefts (S01-S09), peaks at the haul and the $6,700 (S10-S11), a beat of stillness on "Khoan" (S13), calm and warm for the analogy (S15-S17), punch on the CCTV cut (S19), a comic button at the end.
