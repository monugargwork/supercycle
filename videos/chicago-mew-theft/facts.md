# Old Skool TCG theft (Chicago): facts
Sources:
- https://www.fox32chicago.com/news/pokemon-cards-worth-6700-stolen-from-chicago-shop-just-months-after-opening (Kasey Chronis, 20 Nov 2026)
- https://abc7chicago.com/post/chicago-crime-4-pokemon-cards-worth-10000-stolen-old-skool-tcg-trading-card-shop-lawndale-milwaukee-avenue/20057796/
- https://www.themarysue.com/chicago-card-shop-owner-says-a-man-sold-him-cards-then-pocketed-4-pokemon-cards-worth-6500-from-a-box-by-the-register/ (citing CBS Chicago)

## Confirmed
- **Theft:** Wednesday 18 Nov 2026, around 4 p.m. (Fox32).
- **Shop and owner:** owner Nicholas "Nick" Bruni has sold online since 2009 and opened the physical shop in September (Fox32). It's on the 3000 block of N. Milwaukee Ave. The neighbourhood differs by outlet: Logan Square, Avondale, or Lawndale/Milwaukee.
- **Sequence (Fox32):**
  1. The man phoned ahead offering sealed Pokémon product and arrived while the shop was busy.
  2. He said he'd look around and took one card from a box of new inventory on the counter by the register.
  3. He sold the product to Bruni for about $300.
  4. He went back to the same box and took three more cards.
- **Cameras and distance:** the cameras were prominently displayed, and Bruni was feet away.
- **Cards:**
  - raw NM Red RGB Mew (30th anniversary), $4,000;
  - PSA 8 Scream promo Psyduck, $1,800;
  - CGC Pristine 10 XY Evolutions M Charizard EX, $750;
  - PSA 10 Celebrations Mew, $150.
- **Total:** $6,700 (Fox32). ABC7 says about $10k and CBS about $6,500.
- **Police:** Bruni filed a report right away and gave police the phone number the man used. It's classified as retail theft. No arrest reported.
- **Quotes (Bruni):** "Normally, I wouldn't have such a valuable box of cards just sitting out." "I was completely shocked that it happened."
