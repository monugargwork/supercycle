# Golden Chocobo private sale: beat sheet (stage 1)

VO: voice.mp3 → voice.wav (Trung Caha, 75.4 s, 2261 f at 30 fps; the reel runs 2275 f). Words: words.json (aligned to script.md; all `check:` lines are digits or spellings whisper wrote differently: 1/một, 47, 77, 10, 250.000, Collector/Collectr, Kai/Cai, ni lông/nilon, chăm/chằm).
Build: the house flat cartoon cards in the inked 3D world, real card scans wherever a card shows, captions burned in, the Supercycle logo top right, meme sounds only where the sound is the joke.

## Logline
Someone pays $1.4M in a back room for a "golden chicken": the serialized Golden Chocobo Magic card, PSA 10. The community squints at a price nearly six times the old record, until the reason shows (graded perfect vs. raw, 77 copies), and still the whole store is pressed against the glass, waiting for the bird to prove it.

## Spine
1. $1.4M paid for a "golden chicken": it's a Magic card, Golden Chocobo 47/77, PSA 10.
2. Collectr posts it: a private sale, confirmed, seller Adam Cai.
3. The community frowns: the old record was only $250k, and this jumped ~6x in a private handshake, no auction, no public invoice.
4. The reason: the old record card was raw; this one is a PSA 10, and only 77 exist, perfect ones far fewer (two cars, one still with plastic on the seats).
5. A creator met the seller at MagicCon Atlanta: the evidence leans real.
6. But a private sale has no hammer, no hall, only a post, so everyone is still staring at the golden chicken.

## Hero objects
- **The slab:** the REAL serialized Golden Chocobo scan (Traveling Chocobo, FIN 551f, `chocobo_gold`, new in the library) in a PSA-style case, label "FINAL FANTASY · TRAVELING CHOCOBO · 47/77 · GEM MT 10" (`reuse:slabReal`). It lives in a brass birdcage.
- **The birdcage:** new, brass, domed, 35 cm wide x 55 cm tall, a door on a hinge, a perch-stand inside that holds the slab upright. A burgundy velvet cloth covers it at the start. This is the "chicken".
- **The cash tower:** strapped cash bricks (16 x 7 x 4 cm, one brick = $50,000) on a store play table. 5 bricks = $250k; 28 bricks = $1.4M. Price = how tall the stack stands.
- **The hen:** a real brown hen (new flat toon card, same ink style as the cast) who wanders the back room. She is the literal "gà thật" and the last-frame gag.

## Hero moments ★
- **S01 hook:** the buyer slams a briefcase on the table on "triệu"; the lid springs open on cash bricks. On "gà vàng" the seller whips the velvet cloth off the cage: gold light floods out, three loose golden feathers puff up off the cage floor and drift down while the camera already pushes in.
- **S09 the tower:** 23 bricks drop one after another onto the 5-brick stack (rigid bodies, each landing with a thud), the tower wobbles to 1.12 m over the table, towering over the three doubters. Then the camera tilts up the tower and through the back-room window, where the handshake happens: no gavel, a blank receipt pad.
- **S17 the stare:** the slab in its cage under the lamp; the camera pulls back through the back-room window to reveal the whole store pressed against the glass, every face on the bird. The hen hops onto the table, pecks the cage once and clucks on "thế".

## Sets and journeys
1. **Back room** (the private sale): a 3 x 3.5 m stockroom behind the game store. One green-shaded lamp low over a small oak table, two chairs, stacked shipping boxes, an interior window (1.4 x 0.9 m) looking into the store. Dim, warm, a pool of light. The hen.
2. **Game store** (the community): play tables with playmats and decks, a counter with a grading lamp, a lightbox world map on the wall. Bright and busy. Its back wall holds the window into the back room (same window, the shared world).
3. **Garage** (the "you see" set): two identical yellow 1990s hatchbacks side by side under a strip light. Cool, calm.
4. **MagicCon Atlanta show floor:** a vendor booth with a banner, aisle carpet, a brass jeweller's balance on the booth table. Bright, convention light.
- Route: back room (hook, cage, macro on the label, phone post) → travel through the window into the store → the doubters, the tower → tilt up the tower and through the window to the handshake → (cut) the squinting doubter → counter (raw vs. PSA 10) → glide to the map → (dissolve 8, slab rectangle → windshield) garage → (dissolve 8) MagicCon → (dissolve 8) back room again, empty → pull back through the window to the staring store.

## Cast

- **The three doubters** (green hoodie, yellow jacket with a purple beanie, purple jacket): `reuse`. Magic players at a play table.
- **Shopkeeper** (green apron, glasses): the counter and the map; he is also the man at the garage (same look, sleeves rolled).
- **Creator** (red cap, black tee, a vlog camera on a short grip with a red REC light): MagicCon only.
- **Store crowd** for S17 (6-8 players, existing outfits recoloured).
- **The hen** (new).

## Visual laws
- **Price = the height of the cash tower** (one brick = $50,000).
- **Graded vs. raw = a hard PSA case with a GEM MT 10 label vs. a bare card in a thin penny sleeve** with a soft, slightly whitened corner.
- **Scarcity = bulbs on the lightbox map:** 77 gold bulbs; on "hoàn hảo" all but 3 go dark.
- **Evidence = the balance:** what's laid on it sinks the pan with the brass plate REAL.
- **Private = behind glass:** the deal is only ever seen through the back-room window or inside that one small lit room.

## Gags
- "một con gà vàng" → the velvet cloth comes off a birdcage.
- "Không phải gà thật" → a real hen beside the table tilts her head and clucks (Chicken meme).
- "Cho con chim này à?" → green hoodie holds the regular Traveling Chocobo from his own deck up to the lens (real scan, `chocobo_plain`).
- "nhảy gần gấp sáu lần" → the cash tower literally jumps up brick by brick.
- "còn nguyên lớp nilon trên ghế" → the car door opens on seats still wrapped in clear plastic; it crinkles when the shopkeeper sits.
- "không có búa, không có khán phòng" → an empty gavel block with no gavel; the pull-back shows two chairs and boxes, no audience.
- "nhìn chằm chằm vào con gà vàng" → everyone pressed to the glass, and the hen has the last word.

## Energy map, light
- Loud, warm-noir hook in the back room (one lamp, gold spill from the cage).
- The label macro is the first calm: slow push, rack focus.
- The store is bright day; the tower is the loudest physical beat.
- The cut to the squinting doubter is the turn ("Khoan đã").
- Counter, map and garage are the calm "you see" stretch (cool garage light).
- MagicCon is bright and busy; the balance settles the question for now.
- The ending goes back to the dim back room; 8 f of stillness before the pull-back, then the hen.
