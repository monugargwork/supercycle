# Pokémon GPS tracker: facts

## Sources
- Aftermath (Nicole Carpenter, 22 Sep 2026): https://aftermath.site/pokemon-scalper-gps-tracker-guilty/
- Dexerto (sentence): https://www.dexerto.com/pokemon/pokemon-collector-who-gps-tracked-a-card-delivery-driver-sentenced-to-wear-a-tracker-himself-3412512/
- Jalopnik: https://www.jalopnik.com/2267936/pokemon-card-scalper-charged-put-gps-tracker-delivery-drivers-vehicle/

## Confirmed
- Kent Kubasta, 72, Ohio; case filed by Wooster police, Wayne County Municipal Court. (Aftermath, Dexerto)
- Victim: a woman who delivers Pokémon cards to stores ("a carrier/vendor of Pokemon cards"); name redacted. (Dexerto, Aftermath)
- Device: a Spytec GPS tracker that pairs with a phone app for real-time location. (Aftermath)
- She found it on her van on 18 June 2026 while delivering to a Walmart and reported it to police. (Dexerto, Jalopnik)
- He admitted placing it to track her location and where she delivered Pokémon cards (to find restocks). (Aftermath, Jalopnik)
- Charge: illegal use of a tracking device, first-degree misdemeanour. Pleaded not guilty, changed to no contest on 31 Aug; found guilty (Monday 21 Sep per Aftermath). (Aftermath, Dexerto)
- Sentence: 12 months community control (probation); first 90 days wearing a GPS monitor; no contact with the driver; $500 fine + court costs; $25/month supervision; violation can mean 6 months jail. (Dexerto, Jalopnik)
- Context: demand at an all-time high; the 30th anniversary set brought overnight lines and fights over places; scalpers wait at Target, Best Buy, Walmart for restocks; restock info is traded in Discord and Facebook groups. (Aftermath)
