# Pokémon GPS tracker: beat sheet (stage 1)

73.6 s · 2209 frames at 30 fps · the voiceover is fixed.

## Logline
A 72-year-old collector skips the overnight Pokémon lines by hiding a GPS tracker on the card delivery driver's van, until she finds it and the court straps one on him instead.

## Spine (cause and effect)
1. He secretly fixes a tracker to the card driver's van.
2. Everyone else fights over every restock; he watches a green dot from his armchair.
3. On 18 June the driver finds the box and takes it to the police; he admits it.
4. **The turn:** restock times are secret, and only the driver knows where and when.
5. The court finds him guilty: probation, a fine, no contact.
6. **The flip:** a tracker on *his* ankle; now he's the dot.

## Hero objects
- **The tracker:** a matchbox-sized black plastic box (9 × 5 × 2.5 cm) with a magnet back and **one green LED that blinks once a second**. It snaps onto the van, she rips it off, it goes into an evidence bag, and it returns as the ankle monitor.
- **His phone:** the map with the green dot.
- **The white delivery van:** stacked with sealed Pokémon boxes.

## Hero moments ★
- **S01 The hook:** at night he creeps to the van; the magnet box leaps the last few centimetres and **clacks** onto the steel on "gắn".
- **S08 The discovery:** she drops her pen, bends under the bumper and sees the green blink. She yanks; the magnet resists, then pops off. She holds the box up into the lens.
- **S17 The ankle monitor:** an officer clamps a black strap round his ankle, **clack** on "đeo", and the same green LED starts to blink.

## Visual law
**One green blink, everywhere.** The tracker's LED, the dot on the phone, the van seen from above and the ankle monitor all blink green at the same rate. The viewer learns "green blink = being followed" in the first five seconds, so the last line needs no explaining.

- he sips tea, perfectly calm, while the chaos happens across town;
- he counts out the 500 dollars one bill at a time onto the clerk's tray;
- final frame: the green dot shuffles slowly along the map, at an old man's walking pace.
