# iCaterpie lifetime ban: facts
Sources:
- https://www.dexerto.com/pokemon/pokemon-tcg-veteran-says-he-was-banned-for-life-after-calling-out-judge-3419510/
- https://gaminghq.eu/2026/10/18/pokemon-tcg-icaterpie-permanently-banned/
- https://gamerant.com/pokemon-tcg-player-icaterpie-banned-why/

## Confirmed
- **Who:** Alessandro "iCaterpie" Cremascoli, Italian. Two-time national champion (Dexerto only). He has taught the game across Italy and built community for 25+ years.
- **The post:** a complaint about a judge at the 2026 Worlds in San Francisco, posted more than a month after Worlds (Gamerant). He named the judge, and deleted the post about 6 hours later (all three outlets).
- **The announcement:** on 16 Oct 2026 he announced on X a permanent ban from Play! Pokémon events. It came as he was about to travel to the Nice Regional (17-18 Oct).
- **TPC:** no statement. The ban's cause is his own account only (Dexerto).
- **Quotes (Dexerto):** "I'm devastated. Pokemon is literally my life. I can't stop crying." "I dedicated 25 years of my life to this game."
- **Community reaction:** split. Some call the ban too harsh; others say he shouldn't have named a judge publicly (Gamerant, JenTCG via Dexerto).
