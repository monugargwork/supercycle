## Logline
An Italian veteran deletes a post about a Worlds judge after six hours and, by his own account, is banned from official Pokémon events for life. The delete button only cleans your own screen: in six hours, everyone has a copy.

2. Who: Alessandro Cremascoli, "iCaterpie", Italian, two-time national champion. 25 years teaching strangers across Italy, a whole community.

8. Silence: The Pokémon Company has said nothing; all we know is his own account.
9. Payoff: the post lived six hours; the ban, he says, is forever.
