## Logline
In a Monaco saleroom that sells yachts, diamonds and watches, a sealed 2006 French Pokémon booster box is crowned like royalty; the reason is wine logic (every opened box is gone forever), so the kid whose mum forbade him to open his should go home and say sorry.

## Spine
1. Monaco sells yachts, diamonds, watches, and now a sealed Pokémon box lands on the table.
2. Hôtel des Ventes de Monte-Carlo, 17 Nov, its first Pokémon sale: 70 lots, gentlemen in suits ready to bid for Pikachu.
3. The top lot: a sealed 36-pack French EX Espèces Delta box (2006) under glass, estimate €130-180k ("I bought that with breakfast money!").
4. Beside it a Poncho Pikachu (Rayquaza) PSA 10, €18-20k: ten times your motorbike.
5. Why? It's wine: every opened bottle, every torn box, is gone forever; the rarer, the fiercer the bidding.
6. So: if mum stopped you opening your box, go home and apologise.

## Hero objects
- **The box:** a built 3D booster display box, upright 20 x 28 x 11 cm, French "ESPÈCES DELTA · 36 BOOSTERS · 2006" printing with the REAL Rayquaza δ scan as its cover art, a glossy shrink-wrap layer that gleams. The same box sits in the vitrine, on every cellar shelf, and on top of mum's cupboard.
- **The vitrine:** brass-edged glass case on a white plinth, a hinged glass front door; a small gold crown is set on top.
- **The slab:** the REAL Poncho Pikachu (Rayquaza poncho, 231/XY-P) in a PSA-style GEM MT 10 case on its own stand.

## Hero moments ★
- **S01 hook:** the gavel slams on "Monaco" (frame 3) over a model yacht; the camera glides down the lot table past a diamond and a gold watch, and a porter thumps the Pokémon box down on "bóc" (f257), the diamond and watch hopping.
- **S15 the torn boxes:** locked frame on the cellar shelf of 18 sealed boxes while the year plate rolls 2006 → 2026; one by one 17 boxes rip open (wrap shreds, lid flips up), the shreds pile on the floor; one sealed box is left gleaming.
- **S17-S18 the ending:** 2006, mum snatches the box from the kid's fingers and puts it on top of the cupboard; dissolve to 2026, the grown kid walks in with flowers and bows to mum; tilt up to the box, still sealed.

## Sets and journeys
1. **Saleroom** (Hôtel des Ventes): cream panelled room, red carpet, a stage with the rostrum, the velvet lot table, the vitrine and the slab stand, a lot board on the back wall; three rows of gold chairs; tall windows on the harbour side; a long display table of 70 small lots along that wall.
2. **Quay** (outside the same wall): the cream façade with "HÔTEL DES VENTES DE MONTE-CARLO" lettering, a stone quay with palms, yachts moored in the harbour. Golden afternoon.
3. **Cellar** (the "you see" set): stone vault, a rack wall (bottles left, sealed boxes right), a barrel table, a year plate. Warm light.
4. **Kitchen** (2006 golden, 2026 day): table, tall cupboard, stove, window, a door on the right.
- Route: saleroom (hook) → pull back through the window to the quay → fly back in through the window along the 70 lots → the gentlemen → the vitrine → (cut) the shouting man → (dissolve) 2006 kitchen → (dissolve) the slab → scooters down the aisle → the vitrine and the waiter → (dissolve, box → shelf) cellar → (dissolve) 2006 kitchen → (dissolve) 2026 kitchen.

## Cast
- Auctioneer (black suit, red tie, grey hair, gavel). Porter (white jacket, black tie). Waiter / sommelier (black waistcoat, bow tie).
- The gentlemen: 18 in dark suits, seated, paddles; **Minh** (navy suit, brown hair, moustache) in row 1 is the breakfast-money man and the grown kid.
- Kid Minh (2006: blue tee, red cap, kid build). Mum (2006: green dress, dark hair; 2026: same dress, grey hair).

## Visual laws
- **Scarcity = sealed vs opened:** a sealed box has glossy wrap and a closed lid; an opened one has its lid up, wrap torn, and it stays opened. Time prop: the cellar year plate.
- **Royal treatment = the crown and the spotlight** (vitrine; the slab gets a spotlight too).
- **Price = count of everyday objects:** ten scooters down the aisle for "ten times your motorbike".

## Gags
- "đắt hơn cả căn nhà" (watches) → a gold watch on a stand with a price tag €2,500,000.
- "giơ bảng vì Pikachu" → 18 paddles snap up in unison on "Pikachu".
- "như vương miện hoàng gia" → the porter sets a little gold crown on top of the vitrine; a spotlight snaps on.
- "Hồi đó tôi mua hộp này bằng tiền ăn sáng!" → Minh shoots up, his chair tips over; 2006: kid Minh hugs the box, noodle bowl pushed away, his "ĂN SÁNG" coin tin tipped out empty.
- "đắt hơn chiếc xe máy của bạn chừng mười lần" → one scooter beside the slab, then the crane reveals ten in a row down the aisle.
- "nó chính là rượu vang" → the box sits in a wine-rack cubby among bottles.
- "xin lỗi mẹ" → the grown man bows with flowers; the box is still sealed on the cupboard.
